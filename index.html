GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Not sure where to begin? Here are a few quick answers. You can also send us a direct enquiry.

What areas does NewHelpline cover?＋

We focus on Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane. Share your neighbourhood in the enquiry form so the team can understand your location.

Can I enquire for online tutoring?＋

Yes. Choose online classes in the form and mention the subject, class level and preferred timings.

I’m a teacher. Can I share my details?＋

Choose the Teacher tab and share your subjects, experience and availability. The message will be prepared in WhatsApp for you to send.

How does the enquiry form work?＋

The form prepares a WhatsApp message using the details you enter. Review the message and tap Send in WhatsApp to contact the team. The website does not store your submission.
