CANDIDATE FAQs

Know before
you apply.

Some quick answers to common candidate questions.

Does submitting a profile guarantee a job?＋

No. Registering your details lets you express interest and share your background. Interviews and placements depend on available opportunities and employer requirements.

What information should I provide?＋

Share your contact information, qualifications, preferred role, experience, skills and location preferences. Avoid sending passwords, banking details or unnecessary identity documents.

What happens after I submit the form?＋

The form prepares a WhatsApp message that you can review and send to NewHelpline. The current website does not store applications in a database, so sending the message is necessary to contact the team.

Can freshers apply?＋

Yes. Choose Fresher / entry level in the experience field and describe your education, skills, projects, internships or other relevant experience.

Where can I see hiring information?＋

Visit the dedicated Hiring & Jobs page for guidance on exploring opportunities and sharing role requirements or candidate profiles.
