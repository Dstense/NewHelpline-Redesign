WHAT NEWHELPLINE DOES

Recruitment starts
with understanding.

NewHelpline provides a starting point for recruitment conversations. Candidates share their career background and preferences; employers share what a role requires. This information helps clarify the need and gives the team a basis for discussing potential fit where an opportunity is available.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Important details for candidates and employers.

Are all vacancies listed on the website?＋

This version of the site provides hiring information and candidate registration. It does not currently publish a live vacancy database. Contact NewHelpline to ask about relevant opportunities.

Does a candidate profile guarantee an interview?＋

No. Profile submission is an expression of interest. Interview selection and placements depend on employer requirements and opportunity availability.

Is an enquiry saved automatically?＋

No. The current forms prepare WhatsApp messages. The user must review and send the message; this static website has no connected database or candidate tracking system.
